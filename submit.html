To receive form submissions in your Google Spreadsheet with uploaded concept paper documents safely stored in Google Drive:

Quick 1-Minute Setup with Google Apps Script:

  1. Open your Google Sheet at sheets.new (or your existing sheet).
  2. Go to Extensions > Apps Script and paste the contents of google-apps-script.js.
  3. Click Deploy > New deployment > Web app.
  4. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone (crucial).
  5. Copy the Web App URL (starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/.../exec) and paste it below!

Once saved, submissions and file uploads flow directly into your Google Spreadsheet in real time.
