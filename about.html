PITCH COMPETITION

What is SYNTHEX 2026?

SYNTHEX 2026 is a technology-focused pitch competition designed to inspire innovation, creativity, and critical thinking among undergraduates. The competition provides a platform for students to present their ideas, explore emerging technologies, and propose solutions to real-world challenges.

By encouraging technical exploration and entrepreneurial thinking, SYNTHEX aims to transform creative concepts into meaningful possibilities and empower the next generation of innovators to shape a better future through technology.

STUDENT ACADEMIC SOCIETY

What is STS?

The Society of Technological Studies (STS) is a student society affiliated with the Department of Physical Sciences and Technology, Faculty of Applied Sciences, Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka. STS aims to foster technological awareness, knowledge sharing, and innovation among undergraduates.

Through academic initiatives, collaborative activities, and technology-focused events, the society encourages students to develop their skills, explore emerging fields, and connect their academic knowledge with practical applications.
